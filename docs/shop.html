Questions.

Are these official keys?

Yes. The key on a Coding card is issued by DeepSeek; the keys on Design and Film cards are issued by BytePlus, ByteDance's international cloud. You use them at the providers' own addresses, with their own model IDs, so your requests never pass through Tokard. Your card shows the key, the base URL and the model ID.

What exactly is a card?

Credit for one model, with its own API key. The face value is the most the key can ever spend. Use one card per app or project, so budgets and keys stay separate.

What happens when a card runs out?

Calls with its key stop with a clear "card empty" error. Top it up from your wallet, or move the app to another card. Nothing is ever charged beyond the face value.

Do cards expire?

No. Credit stays on the card until you use it. Flip offers last 24 hours; the card you buy from one does not.

Is the flip a lottery?

No. Flipping is free and you never pay to flip. The flip only shows a price for one card; you see it before you decide, and the odds are printed on the Flip page.
