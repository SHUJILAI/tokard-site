The rules.

  1. Flipping is free. There is no way to pay for a flip, a re-roll or better odds.
  2. Three cards per account each day, one per deck. New cards at 00:00 UTC; each offer stays open for 24 hours.
  3. You see the price first. Checkout always shows the same card and price the flip showed.
  4. 20 jackpots a day, sitewide. Once they are gone, a jackpot draw becomes a Rare card.
  5. Gold within five flips. A gold card costs less than 75% of its face value. If four flips in a row miss gold, the fifth is a Lucky card at 70% of face value. Daily flips and invite flips both count, and the count carries over from day to day.
  6. One Ultimate for every 10,000 cards sold: a $100 card for $0.10, turned over like any other card. To claim it, an account needs $20 of purchases; unclaimed after 24 hours, it goes back into the draw. One per account every 30 days.
  7. Invite a friend, flip again. When someone you invite signs up and flips their first card, you get one extra flip: up to 3 a day, 10 held. Invites from your own devices or accounts don't count.
  8. Every card is also sold at a fixed price in the shop, any day.
  9. Card credit never expires and has no cash value.
